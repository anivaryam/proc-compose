### Inferences

#### Strongest coherent competing recommendation

**Preserve the working test-owned harness; first find the owner's actual friction, rather than make retention the initial intervention.** The inherited record reports successful cleanup and later green gates, while external-endpoint/reset compatibility is unknown. That supports a short owner walkthrough or prospective command trace of the current workflow, plus independently confirmed documentation corrections, before spending the pilot budget on transferring lifecycle ownership. A recipe should be tested only for a named ambiguity that the existing guide does not already resolve. This is an alternative sequencing decision, not a claim that the existing harness is generally superior. [Earlier coverage:22–24,30,43–44](../Local%20development%20agent%20opportunities/local%20workflow%20and%20coverage.md#L22-L44); [report:56–57,73](../../reports/Agent%20workflow%20opportunities%20and%20validation.md#L56-L73).

The strongest **feature-forward** alternative is a narrow, optional **incarnation-conditional structured mutation contract**: machine-readable restart/reload outcomes, and an expected-run precondition enforced with mutation admission, preserving generation waits and partial-failure semantics. Its rationale would be preventive correctness and dependable parsing, not unmeasured startup speed. A known discovery-to-mutation race can justify a narrow safety contract if the owner requires it; waiting for the race to happen during two unrelated edits is not inherently necessary. However, this study establishes neither incident frequency nor user priority, so it supports an acceptance-specification candidate, not implementation approval or the claim that this feature wins. [Baseline:47,56](../Agent%20workflow%20opportunities%20and%20validation/proc-compose-baseline.md#L47-L56); [earlier coverage:86–89](../Local%20development%20agent%20opportunities/local%20workflow%20and%20coverage.md#L86-L89).

This alternative leaves the core conclusion intact: **do not add MCP, a parallel check engine or a retained-check API merely because the contracts can be described.** It challenges the certainty and sequencing of “recipes first,” not the value of reuse.

## 3. Can the three-edit pilot falsify its hypotheses, and what is the smallest adequate correction?

### Takeaway

**Yes for local compatibility and concrete safety counterexamples; only directionally for usability, and not for general product demand or absence of feature value.** Three real edits are a reasonable feasibility budget. The necessary changes are clearer estimands, exposure counts, observations and decision labels—not a randomized controlled trial or a larger mandatory matrix.

#### F4 — Equal counts are local non-improvement, not general disconfirmation

- **Severity / classification:** **low — overstatement** in falsification language; the order/sample limitations themselves are substantially acknowledged.
- **Exact locations:** `research/reports/Agent workflow opportunities and validation.md:75–77`; `research/research_notes/Agent workflow opportunities and validation/agent-workflows.md:91,96`. Randomizing which complete block runs first gives only one order realization; it does not counterbalance learning, agent context, model variation, cache effects or host conditions. Equalize pre-block baselines without erasing B's intended within-block warm reuse. #### F5 — “Two of three” uses edits as the wrong failure denominator and can produce false no-feature conclusions

- **Severity / classification:** **medium — overstatement in the decision inference**; the proposed threshold is a policy choice, not an observed finding.
- **Exact locations:** `research/reports/Agent workflow opportunities and validation.md:71,79–81`; inherited threshold at `reports/Local development agent opportunities.md:49`; note threshold at `research/research_notes/Agent workflow opportunities and validation/alternatives-and-opportunities.md:86`. - **Severity / classification:** **medium — limitation of the proposed safety evaluation**, not a reported false pass.

### Inferences

#### Smallest coherent pilot specification after these corrections

This is a proposed clarification of the existing pilot, not authorization or a replacement implementation plan:

1. **Name the actual question and controlling design.** Use final-report A/B for a bundle-feasibility decision, not a documentation-only effect or a benchmark of a future feature. Freeze the exact currently working A commands/ownership and installed versions; do not assume a historical report establishes today's baseline. If the owner only wants to fix discoverability, prefer the smaller same-workflow guide comparison instead. [Report:71–77](../../reports/Agent%20workflow%20opportunities%20and%20validation.md#L71-L77); F2–F3.
2. **Keep compatibility first.** Confirm external endpoint support, guarded resets, application identity and browser/shell access without constructing a competing launcher. Failure preserves the current harness and rejects this retained integration for this job; it does not settle the product's entire opportunity space. [Report:73](../../reports/Agent%20workflow%20opportunities%20and%20validation.md#L73); F5.
3. **Bind three real edits and their expected observations before replay.** Exact revisions, generated/build prerequisites, fixture policy, assertions, expected failures, permissions and starting context must be equivalent. Replaying recorded edits tests operational workflow, not the productivity of discovering those edits. Record one-time recipe/integration preparation separately from recurring loop cost; do not hide migration work in a “low effort” label. [Report:51,71,75–77](../../reports/Agent%20workflow%20opportunities%20and%20validation.md#L51-L77); F1, F4.
4. **Use one randomized block per arm if that is the budget.** Restore equivalent pre-block snapshots/caches where feasible; B remains warm within its block. Capture end-to-end monotonic time, initial/final lifecycle, restarts, test time, actual overlap, tool calls and interventions. Report three matched edit positions within two blocks, not six independent experimental units or a causal speed estimate. Define retry/intervention counting and note floor effects. [Report:75–77](../../reports/Agent%20workflow%20opportunities%20and%20validation.md#L75-L77); F4.
5. **Specify only the safety exposures needed for the claim.** A bounded normal/interrupted child-cleanup check with explicit identities, postconditions and unrelated control can challenge local cleanup feasibility. A known identity mismatch is necessary only if asserting rejection; otherwise list identity rejection as untested. The optional two-worktree gate remains separate and cannot confer general data isolation. No claim about incomplete-reap handling, platform breadth or all escaped descendants follows unless that exact boundary is observed. [Report:79–83](../../reports/Agent%20workflow%20opportunities%20and%20validation.md#L79-L83); F6.
6. **Predeclare a scoped decision rule.** No unsafe pass or foreign mutation is acceptable; abort/investigate rather than repeat unsafe behavior. Agree any practical time threshold and eligible exposure denominator. Successful B with fewer interventions supports this bundle on this job; equal counts mean no observed count benefit. Adequate A or failed compatibility means no implementation selected from this pilot. A reproducible narrow contract failure can support a separately approved proposal; it need not fail in two unrelated edits or two projects to be real. [Report:81,85–89](../../reports/Agent%20workflow%20opportunities%20and%20validation.md#L81-L89); F4–F6.

**Rejected alternatives:** requiring a large randomized trial, merging every note's scenarios into one pilot, or demanding broad interviews before correcting a factual doc error would be disproportionate. Conversely, exposing a new runner/API merely to manufacture a favorable comparison would contradict the study's legitimate reuse and compatibility constraints. The smallest useful outcome remains an attributable local observation and a scoped decision, including no implementation. [Report:63,65,73,85–89](../../reports/Agent%20workflow%20opportunities%20and%20validation.md#L63-L89).
