## Findings narrow operational claims and pilot decisions

### AV1 — Medium: escalation counts the wrong opportunities

**Methodological overstatement.** The [original report:81](Agent%20workflow%20opportunities%20and%20validation.md#L81) requires a deficit in “at least two of three iterations,” inherited from the [earlier report:49](Local%20development%20agent%20opportunities.md#L49). Three edit labels do not supply three exposures to restart parsing, stale-incarnation mutation or incomplete reap. One eligible exposure cannot meet that threshold; an unsafe observation correctly aborts before repetition. Count **eligible exposures per contract**, investigate one serious safety failure immediately, and reproduce non-dangerous deficits only with separate approval. “No feature” must mean no implementation selected **for this job on this evidence**, not no unmet need. The strongest counterargument is that these are explicitly proposed policy thresholds, not observations; demanding reproducibility remains sensible. The correction changes escalation eligibility, not the presumption against speculative work ([methodology review:171–178](../research_notes/Adversarial%20review%20of%20agent%20research/methodology-review.md#L171-L178)).

### AV2 — Medium: zero observed failures needs an exposure and oracle

**Pilot-specification limitation, not a false reported pass.** [Original report:79–83](Agent%20workflow%20opportunities%20and%20validation.md#L79-L83) requires identity/cleanup observations and one interruption, but does not prescribe a known identity mismatch, equal interruption opportunities, an ownership inventory or a post-check observation window. Ordinary edits cannot establish rejection of wrong identity. An unrelated-process control tests non-interference, not whether the observer detects survivors. Predeclare exposures, independently observed postconditions and unknown outcomes; omitted boundaries stay **not exercised**. This matters before accepting safety claims. Severity is limited because the original already rejects exit-code-only proof, demands immediate safety aborts and distinguishes per-check cleanup from final shutdown ([methodology review:180–187](../research_notes/Adversarial%20review%20of%20agent%20research/methodology-review.md#L180-L187)).

### AV3 — Medium: `verify --json` has a parser exception

**Confirmed error in the unqualified assertion.** [Original report:20](Agent%20workflow%20opportunities%20and%20validation.md#L20) says it “emits one bounded object.” The [flag-error handler:311–318](../../cmd/proc-compose/verify.go#L311-L318) instead writes plaintext stderr; an [existing assertion:109–112](../../cmd/proc-compose/verify_cli_test.go#L109-L112) expects empty stdout and exit 2 for `verify --json --bogus -- true`. The assertion was read, not executed. Say normal operation/semantic results use bounded JSON, while parser errors can leave stdout empty; clients must inspect exit/stderr too. Successful JSON behavior is not disproved, and no new transport follows. This corrects recipe/parser expectations ([repository review:17–23](../research_notes/Adversarial%20review%20of%20agent%20research/repository-verification.md#L17-L23)).

### AV4 — Medium: Playwright refuses available endpoints, not every listener

**Overbroad operational assertion.** [Original agent-workflows.md:48](../research_notes/Agent%20workflow%20opportunities%20and%20validation/agent-workflows.md#L48) says `reuseExistingServer: false` rejects an already-listening server. In URL mode, [Playwright's guide](https://playwright.dev/docs/test-webserver) and the source reviewer's inspected upstream availability branch instead require its accepted HTTP response; a listener returning 500 need not trigger refusal before attempted launch. Correct this to **reuse/refusal follows the configured availability test**, not blanket port ownership or checkout identity. No collision was reproduced; upstream `main` is not installed-version proof. The guide itself uses loose “listening” wording, and the [original report:26](Agent%20workflow%20opportunities%20and%20validation.md#L26) already rejects identity inference; those counterarguments limit this to a safety-recipe correction, not a demonstrated wrong-run incident ([agent-source review:54–62](../research_notes/Adversarial%20review%20of%20agent%20research/agent-source-verification.md#L54-L62)).

### AV5 — Low: priority and MCP cost remain design-dependent

**Qualification, not a false measured-cost claim.** The [original report:3,51–61](Agent%20workflow%20opportunities%20and%20validation.md#L3-L61) calls recipes the strongest opportunity and MCP's ongoing burden high; [alternatives-and-opportunities.md:52–58](../research_notes/Agent%20workflow%20opportunities%20and%20validation/alternatives-and-opportunities.md#L52-L58) invokes an always-on server. [process-compose's MCP documentation](https://f1bonacc1.github.io/process-compose/mcp-server/) documents stdio, which avoids a network listener, as well as SSE. Neither proves comparative maintenance cost. **CLI/documentation/integration is a low-cost default hypothesis, not the proven best opportunity.** MCP is not uniquely differentiating but can offer typed discovery/invocation; cost depends on transport, authority and contract reuse. Keep the client/demand gate, not a fixed cost rationale. Downgraded from the reviewer's medium candidate because qualitative rankings and usability-based reconsideration are already explicit ([alternatives review:108–116](../research_notes/Adversarial%20review%20of%20agent%20research/alternatives-verification.md#L108-L116)).

## One repaired pilot tests bundle feasibility only

**Proposed clarification, not execution authority:** use this single design for the selected pilot, replacing—not combining—the fresh-verify, six-scenario and two-project/12-case variants in [baseline:80](../research_notes/Agent%20workflow%20opportunities%20and%20validation/proc-compose-baseline.md#L80), [agent note:89–96](../research_notes/Agent%20workflow%20opportunities%20and%20validation/agent-workflows.md#L89-L96) and [alternatives:79–86](../research_notes/Agent%20workflow%20opportunities%20and%20validation/alternatives-and-opportunities.md#L79-L86). Their variation is a handoff ambiguity, not evidence of a misrun experiment. The final report already names its primary pilot and declares bundled differences ([report:71–77](Agent%20workflow%20opportunities%20and%20validation.md#L71-L77)).

Freeze **A**, the actual working CLI/Playwright workflow, and **B**, existing retained proc-compose plus the same check and concise recipe. Preserve A's legitimate reuse; do not manufacture cold starts. Pin commands, versions and owners; do not assume historical records establish today's baseline. First establish external-endpoint support, guarded-reset compatibility, real-path identity and actual shell/browser access. If retention violates acceptance or requires another harness, reject B for this job.

Bind three real frontend/API-or-worker/follow-up edits to revisions and expected assertions. Equalize source/build prerequisites, fixtures, credentials, permissions, deadlines, agent/model versions, initial contexts/prompts and instrumentation. Record preparation separately. Randomize complete-block order; restore equivalent pre-block snapshots/cache baselines where feasible, documenting exceptions, but preserve B's intended within-block warmth. The estimand is **this integration bundle's acceptability and observed interaction cost**; retention, ownership and guidance cannot receive separate causal credit. Three edits test feasibility, not productivity of discovering changes.

| Predeclared exposure, equally offered to A/B | Required observation / limit |
|---|---|
| Normal check and one approved bounded interruption | Inventory owned PID/start identities, children, endpoints and allowed retained services. Fix deadlines/windows before approval. Independent OS process/port observations must show check children/listeners exit, retained services survive, and final stop releases only owned lifetimes. |
| One safe known identity mismatch | Real application-path build/fixture/worker readback must reject mismatch, not echo supplied expectations. Without a valid oracle, rejection is unverified. |
| Unrelated control; declared survivor-detection check | Control remains untouched; observer must identify the approved owned child/listener while alive and its later exit. Group-only visibility cannot exclude fully detached escapes; lost observability means unknown, not pass. |

Execution remains blocked until owners, oracles, deadlines and observation windows are approved. Record each contract's eligible exposures and observed outcomes, lifecycle-related retry/intervention definitions, tool calls and monotonic ready/restart/check/cleanup spans. Use **complete block wall-clock time**, from before first edit/start through final owned cleanup, never summed concurrent durations. Equal counts can reflect a floor: report “no observed advantage,” not general disconfirmation. Order, learning and cache confounding preclude causal time/productivity attribution.

Abort on unsafe/unverified cleanup, foreign mutation, shared-state damage or bypass; do not repeat harm to satisfy a count. Agree materiality before execution. Outcomes are A adequate, B incompatible/rejected, bundle feasible, boundary deficit reproduced, no advantage observed, or inconclusive. No eligible exposure means inconclusive for that contract. Two-worktree isolation, denied-network/noisy-secret probes, incomplete-reap sealing and platform breadth are deferred unless separately scoped. No-feature decisions concern this selected job, not all future users ([methodology repair](../research_notes/Adversarial%20review%20of%20agent%20research/methodology-review.md#L191-L206)).
